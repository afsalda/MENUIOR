# New-release sample artwork

Four original sample illustrations generated using the **built-in image_gen tool**, one call each, with genuine PNG alpha transparency. These are illustrative food assets, not verified photos of Fress Trees dishes. Replace them with approved cutout product photos for launch.

- `pasta.png` — creamy Alfredo penne in a black bowl.
- `salad.png` — Greek salad on a white plate.
- `latte.png` — iced latte glass.
- `mocktail.png` — watermelon, mint and lime mocktail glass.

The complete prompts are preserved in `prompts.json`. The source video was used to study layout and choreography; none of its branding or food photographs was extracted for the public app. Animation, labels and prices are live HTML/CSS, not baked into the artwork.
