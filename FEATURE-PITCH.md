# Two features to lead with

**A menu that stays current.** Change a price or mark a dish sold out from your phone. Saved changes reach open guest menus in about three seconds, and unavailable dishes cannot be added to an order. No menu reprints when stock or prices change.

**One QR code, four languages.** Let guests browse in Malayalam, English, Hindi or Arabic. A useful fit for Kerala cafés serving local guests and tourists: translated dish names and descriptions, with right-to-left layout for Arabic. The owner edits all four languages in one panel; untranslated content falls back to English.

The full package also includes approved photo uploads, owner-selected bestseller tags, paired-dish suggestions, dish-view analytics, WhatsApp order drafts and waiter requests, and an optional Google review invitation after a bill request.

## Operate it

Open `/?admin=1` and sign in as the configured owner. The Guest experience dashboard links to translation gaps, missing photos, bestsellers, paired dishes, WhatsApp settings and the Google review link. Use Menu items for price and stock changes, Settings for café-wide links, and Dish views for analytics.

Views mean dish-detail opens, not unique visitors or sales. WhatsApp requests are confirmed by the café after the guest sends them. The review invitation needs the café's actual Google review URL. Sample photos, prices and translations need approval before public launch.
